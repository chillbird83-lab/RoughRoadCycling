Featured Route

The Neon
Divide

Distance 350 Miles
Elevation 25,000 ft
Terrain 80% Dirt, 20% Tarmac
Duration 4-6 Days

A punishing but intensely rewarding route traversing the high desert plateaus of Colorado and plunging into dense alpine forests. Expect 50-mile stretches without resupply and technical, bone-rattling descents that require dialed 45mm+ tires. Sourced from local RideWithGPS heatmaps, this is not for the faint of heart. Ensure your sleep system is rated for sub-zero.
