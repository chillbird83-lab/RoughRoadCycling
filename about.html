Persona

The Cold, Hard Truth.

I am Tom Bata, and I break things so you don't have to. The cycling industry is built on hype, marketing fluff, and paid reviews. Out on the Neon Divide, hype doesn't keep your gear dry, and marketing doesn't stop your frame from failing.

THE GRIT SCORE //

Data over dogma.

Every piece of gear on this site is tested using our proprietary Grit Score protocol. This isn't an unboxing video. We take gear out into the harshest conditions—washboard dirt, relentless crosswinds, freezing mud—and log the failure points. A 10/10 doesn't exist. We score based on aerodynamics, stability under load, weather resistance, and survivability.
